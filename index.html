Good to Know

Party Policies & FAQ

Important details regarding booking fees, balances, and safety

How does the Booking Fee and payment work?

A $25 booking fee is due to hold your date. The booking fee is put directly towards your hourly event total and is non-refundable.

*Please allow Lindsey to confirm availability for your date before sending a booking fee!

The remaining balance is due at the start of your event. You may pay using CashApp ($LindseyErinRay), Venmo (@LindseyErinRay), Cash, or Check (payable to Lindsey Ray).

What is your Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy?

• Cancellations over 24 hours prior: Can be rescheduled based on availability. Your $25 booking fee remains non-refundable but transfers to your new date.

• Cancellations under 24 hours: Owe the full remaining balance.

Is Lindsey insured?

Yes! Lindsey carries professional business liability insurance. Proof of insurance can be provided upon request for schools, venues, or community organizations.

What kind of face paint do you use and how is it removed?

Lindsey exclusively uses high-grade, professional, FDA-compliant cosmetic face paints (water-based, hypoallergenic, non-toxic, and specially formulated for gentle skin). It washes off easily with warm water and mild soap or baby shampoo!
